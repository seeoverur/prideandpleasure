About the Community

Respect, Consent & Belonging

BDSM — Bondage & Discipline, Dominance & Submission, Sadism & Masochism — encompasses a broad spectrum of interests, roles, and play styles embraced by consenting adults worldwide.

Community spaces center on connection, mutual respect, informed consent, and shared learning. The leather pride flag and kindred symbols are widely recognized within leather, fetish, and kink subcultures as emblems of identity and belonging.

"The goal is always a safe, sane, and consensual space where every participant feels seen and respected."

— Community Principle
