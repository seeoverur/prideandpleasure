Where to Begin

Online Community Platforms

Many people begin their journey by joining online communities focused on discussion, education, and event discovery rather than explicit content — a safe entry point into a broader world.

🌐

FetLife

A social network for the BDSM, fetish, and kink community. Used to find local events, discussion groups, and community meetups worldwide.

fetlife.com

↗ 📋

Kink Aware Professionals (KAP)

Educational and advocacy resource that supports safe, informed participation in alternative lifestyles — including a directory of kink-aware therapists.

Face to Face

In-Person Socials & Events

Offline events remain one of the most trusted ways to meet others in the community. These spaces prioritize consent, boundaries, and respect — with experienced hosts maintaining community standards.

☕

Munches

Casual, non-play social gatherings held in public venues like cafés or restaurants. Welcoming to newcomers — often listed through local FetLife groups.

Search your city on fetlife.com → Groups

🎓

Workshops & Classes

Educational events focused on skills, communication, safety, and power dynamics. Kink Academy offers online counterparts as well.

kinkacademy.com

↗
🎟

Private & Ticketed Events

Invite-only or vetted spaces that may include performances, demonstrations, or themed nights. Entry is typically moderated to maintain safety and community standards.

Typically accessed via community referral or FetLife

Foundation of Everything

Safety, Consent & Trust

Meeting within the BDSM community is guided by clear, non-negotiable principles that protect every participant. These aren't rules imposed from outside — they're values the community holds for itself.

✅

Informed Consent

Enthusiastic, ongoing agreement from all participants — freely given and reversible at any time.

💬

Clear Communication

Explicit discussion of boundaries, expectations, and desires before any engagement.

🔒

Privacy & Anonymity

Respect for participants' right to discretion — within the community and beyond it.

🤝

Community Accountability

Shared responsibility for maintaining safe spaces and addressing harmful behavior collectively.

National Coalition for Sexual Freedom Educational resources, legal support, and advocacy reinforcing best practices across the community.
